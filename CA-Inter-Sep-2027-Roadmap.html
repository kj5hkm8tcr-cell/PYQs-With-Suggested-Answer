Step 3 · Passing rules & benefits

How you pass, the set-off benefit & exemptions

Basic passing rule (per group)

  • At least 40 marks in every paper.
  • At least 50% aggregate in the group, i.e. 150 / 300.
  • Both conditions must be met to pass that group.

Exemption (60+ rule)

  • If you fail a group but score 60 or more in a paper, you get an exemption in that paper.
  • Next time you write only the remaining papers of that group.

Set-off benefit (both groups together)

  • Applies when you write both groups in the same attempt.
  • You have 40+ in all 6 papers, but one group's aggregate is below 150.
  • If the other group has extra marks above 150, the extra covers the shortfall.
  • Simply put: 300 / 600 total + 40 in every paper = both groups passed.

Step 6 · Strategy that works

Important strategy tips

01

Finish classes in 6–7 months

Classes are not preparation. They come before preparation. If classes run into July, there's no time left to revise.

02

Highlight important questions during classes

Mark PYQ / RTP / MTP-type and tricky questions right away. Those marks become your revision list later.

03

Cut down redundant questions

If 5 questions test the same concept, keep the best 1–2. Revising 300 smart questions beats 900 repeated ones.

04

Plan every revision in advance

For each chapter decide when you'll revise it and how: what you cover in R1 and what you cover in R2. Write it down.

05

Plan for at least 2 revisions

One revision is not enough for 6 papers. Two revisions is the minimum target. A third quick round is a bonus.

06

Keep 2.5–3 months for revision

After classes end you need enough time for 2 revisions. That's why classes must be done by May.
