Step 3 · The big decision

Both groups together, or one group first?

Recommended for most

Both groups (Sep 2027)

  • Finish Inter in one go. Articleship and Final start sooner.
  • About 10 months is enough if you stay consistent.
  • Concepts link up: Accounts ↔ Audit, Costing ↔ FM.
  • Aggregate is per group, so one strong paper can carry a weaker paper in the same group.
  • Needs about 8–10 focused hours a day, 6 papers to revise twice.
Situational

One group first

  • Good if you're in college, working, or started late.
  • Lighter load, so you can aim for higher marks and exemptions.
  • Common route: Group I in Sep 2027, Group II in Jan 2028.
  • Inter takes one more attempt cycle and Final gets pushed back.
Simple rule: If you can give 8+ honest hours a day from now, go for both groups. If not, pick one group and do it really well. Don't take both groups and then half-prepare them.

Step 5 · Strategy that works

Important strategy tips

01

Finish classes in 6–7 months

Classes are not preparation. They come before preparation. If classes run into July, there's no time left to revise.

02

Highlight important questions during classes

Mark PYQ / RTP / MTP-type and tricky questions right away. Those marks become your revision list later.

03

Cut down redundant questions

If 5 questions test the same concept, keep the best 1–2. Revising 300 smart questions beats 900 repeated ones.

04

Plan every revision in advance

For each chapter decide when you'll revise it and how: what you cover in R1 and what you cover in R2. Write it down.

05

Plan for at least 2 revisions

One revision is not enough for 6 papers. Two revisions is the minimum target. A third quick round is a bonus.

06

Keep 2.5–3 months for revision

After classes end you need enough time for 2 revisions. That's why classes must be done by May.

Step 6 · Revision blueprint

How to plan Revision 1 and Revision 2

Revision 1 · Depth

Cover 100%

About 6–7 weeks · June to mid-July
  • 100% concepts and the whole syllabus. Leave nothing out.
  • ICAI study material questions + illustrations.
  • PYQs, RTPs and MTPs, chapter-wise.
  • Make short notes: formulas, sections, SA keywords, mistakes.
Revision 2 · Speed

Only what matters

About 4–5 weeks · mid-July to August
  • Highlighted / important questions only.
  • Your mistake list and short notes.
  • At least 1–2 full papers per subject, timed for 3 hours.
  • Theory: practise writing in exam language.
Revision 3 · Optional

Final touch

Exam gaps / last week
  • Formulas, charts, notes.
  • Amendments in Tax & Law.
  • No new material.

Rule of thumb: you need at least 2.5–3 months after classes to complete 2 proper revisions of 6 papers.

Bonus · Paper-wise pointers

Quick tips for each paper

P1Advanced Accounting

Practice-heavy. Do questions every day. Learn AS concepts with their logic, and present working notes neatly.

P2Corporate & Other Laws

Use ICAI language. Write in a provision, facts, conclusion format. Revise sections often because theory fades fast.

P3Taxation

Study as per the Finance Act applicable to Sep 2027 (check ICAI's notice). Keep up with amendments. Practise IT and GST equally.

P4Cost & Mgmt Accounting

Concepts first, then practice. Keep a formula sheet. Revisit standard costing and marginal costing often.

P5Auditing & Ethics

Read the ICAI module line by line. Learn SA keywords. Practise case-study answers by writing them.

P6FM & SM

FM: formulas plus practice. SM: case-study based, so apply concepts to the case and don't just copy theory.

Every paper has an MCQ part. Practise MCQs from ICAI's MTPs / RTPs. These are easy marks and also show you weak areas.

Bonus · Things nobody tells you

More points for fresh Foundation passouts

  • Inter is a big jump from Foundation. More depth, more theory and subjective answers. Don't carry Foundation-level effort into it.
  • ICAI material + RTP + MTP + PYQ are the base. Faculty notes add to them and don't replace them.
  • One faculty per subject. Switching teachers halfway wastes weeks.
  • Track backlog weekly. A 1-week backlog is easy to fix. A 1-month backlog breaks the plan.
  • Write, don't just read. Presentation, working notes and time management are scored too.
  • Consistency beats intensity. 8 hours every day beats 14 hours on some days and zero on others.
  • Protect sleep and health, and cut down social media. Burnout in July ruins revision.
  • Do ICITSS (ITT + OT) after the exam, in the gap before results, so articleship can start right away.
