Find where it broke: the 4 phases of study

PHASE 1

Conceptual clarity

Tuition + self-study
  • Watched lectures, skipped self-study
  • Backlog piled up
  • Never read the ICAI study material
PHASE 2

Revision

Making it stick
  • Only one revision done
  • Read, but didn't write
  • Avoided weak chapters
PHASE 3

Mock tests

Exam simulation
  • No full 3-hour papers
  • Didn't compare with ICAI suggested answers
  • Ignored presentation & time
PHASE 4

1.5-day revision

Before each paper
  • Started new topics
  • No plan for the hours
  • Skipped sleep, panicked
Why did I fail? Drill down

Go to the root cause, not just the subject

1 · Subject level
Which subject pulled me down?
e.g. Taxation
2 · Chapter level
Which chapter is tricky or difficult?
e.g. Capital Gains
3 · Topic level
Where exactly am I stuck?
e.g. Sec 54 & 54F
